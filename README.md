# chrono
Chronology of Life in the universe 

The universe began 13.8 billion years ago with the Big Bang, creating space and matter, with the first stars appearing hundreds of millions of years later. Earth formed 4.6 billion years ago, with life (prokaryotes) beginning around 3.8 billion years ago. Complex life emerged much later, culminating in modern humans, with the entire history involving cosmic expansion and chemical evolution.
